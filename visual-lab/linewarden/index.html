FIELD GUIDE

The economy is a weapon.

Your current income pays again forever on one fixed, visible 5-second cycle. Buying a hostile raises that recurring amount exactly once and adds it to your permanent roster; every roster body returns gradually in every later wave. Each actual kill pays one immediate bounty only—never a passive bounty. A returning enemy can pay another bounty when killed again because that is a new kill.

Automatic summonsChoose a unit, switch Auto on, and set protected gold. Auto buys at most once after each payday and waits during critical danger.
Levels and gearKills grant XP. Levels grow your base stats and unlock tougher summons; forge items consume the same gold you could invest in income.
Q — Rift DashDash toward your aim, cutting through foes.
E — Prism NovaBlast and slow all nearby enemies.
R — SentinelSummon a temporary turret beside the crystal.
Space — PauseThe wave clock and 5-second payday meter both stop.
