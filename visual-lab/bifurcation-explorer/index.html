05 · Memory in a scalar flow

Hysteresis: the path depends on the past

Load the hysteresis preset to begin

Increasing r Decreasing r

In the bistable interval, the same parameter value supports two attracting equilibria. As r increases, the tracked state stays near the lower branch and tips upward after the right fold; as r decreases, it stays near the upper branch and tips downward after the left fold. The static folds are the quasi-static thresholds. At finite sweep speed, tipping can lag beyond them; the speed control changes that lag and the recorded loop width.

What the random button is doing. The generated families use smooth combinations of low-degree polynomials, trigonometric terms, exponentials, and log(1 + x²). They are structured to contain a visible fold, branch crossing, or symmetry-breaking event; a truly unrestricted random one-parameter family would almost always produce folds and almost never land exactly on a transcritical or pitchfork degeneracy.

What the many-fold examples mean. The four-fold and adjustable n-fold families are genuine analytic scalar differential equations with several equilibrium branches meeting at one parameter value. They are deliberately constructed, high-codimension events—not structurally stable one-parameter bifurcations. A small generic perturbation separates the meeting into simpler events. The imperfection control demonstrates that same idea on a pitchfork: ε = 0 has exact x ↦ −x symmetry, while ε ≠ 0 breaks it.
