    Mathematical and numerical notes

    Which events are generic? In a generic one-parameter family of planar vector fields, the local equilibrium bifurcations are saddle-node and Hopf. Transcritical and pitchfork crossings require an invariant structure or symmetry. Folds of periodic orbits and SNIC onset are global events: they depend on the organization of trajectories beyond a single equilibrium.

    Equilibria, nullclines, and stability. An equilibrium is an intersection of the two nullclines f(x, y; r) = 0 and g(x, y; r) = 0. The eigenvalues of the Jacobian at that point describe local contraction, expansion, and rotation. A saddle has one eigenvalue on each side of the imaginary axis, so it is unstable overall, with one locally attracting and one locally repelling direction.

    Periodic orbits. The supercritical Hopf family has an attracting periodic orbit of radius ρ = √r for r > 0. The bounded subcritical family adds a quintic term that confines the global flow without changing the local Hopf type. For general families, statements about periodic orbits are numerical detections in the displayed window, not proofs that every orbit has been found.

    Parameter sweeps. Each fixed value of r defines an autonomous system. During a sweep, the display changes that system gradually to show the family. A moving particle during the sweep should therefore be read as a visual guide through nearby phase portraits, not as a solution of any one fixed autonomous equation.
