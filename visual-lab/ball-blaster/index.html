Mouse mode keeps the pointer inside the arena. Press Esc to pause and get it back.

How it works. Hit glowing targets to earn points; quick hits extend your combo for larger rewards, and every hit sends its target roaming to a new place. Matching power-ups add both a level and more time. Turbo boots increase running speed, moon jump adds height and distance, Wall Walk lets you keep running onto a wall, and splat balls leave one colorful mark before disappearing. Gravity warnings announce which of the twelve faces will become down next. Comfort mode snaps the view into place while targets keep roaming. Balls remain short-lived and pass through one another so even a maximum-stack barrage stays smooth.
