Visual Lab

Things worth playing with

A growing collection of interactive sketches in graphs, dynamics, and computation—small mathematical worlds to prod, perturb, and watch unfold.

Current experiments

Choose a system and change the rules.

Each visualizer is a live model, not a prerecorded animation. Adjust its parameters, introduce a disturbance, or simply let it run and see what structure emerges.

01 · Graph dynamics

Game of Life on graphs

Run a Life-like cellular automaton on a toroidal grid, a random regular graph, an Erdős–Rényi graph, a preferential-attachment network, or a clustered network. Change the rule, seed a population, rewire edges as it runs, and follow it through three dimensions.

Interactive · WebGL Open visualizer →
02 · Nonlinear control

Cart–double-pendulum swing-up

Watch a cart drive two linked pendulums toward the unstable upright position, hand off to a balancing controller, or take over the actuator and attempt the swing-up yourself.

Interactive · Simulation Open visualizer →
03 · Physics playground

Bouncy Ball Blaster

Roam a bright 3D arena, launch short-lived bouncing balls, and collect temporary upgrades for rapid fire, jumbo shots, and colorful splats.

Interactive · Three.js Enter playground →

An open shelf

More strange little systems will live here.
