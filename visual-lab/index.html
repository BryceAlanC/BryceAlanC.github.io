Interactive demos

Math demos

Mathematical visualizations

Change the controls and watch each simulated system respond. The individual pages include a short explanation of what is being shown.

01 · Graph dynamics

Game of Life on graphs

Run a Life-like cellular automaton on a toroidal grid, a random regular graph, an Erdős–Rényi graph, a preferential-attachment network, or a clustered network. Change the rule, seed a population, rewire edges as it runs, and follow it through three dimensions.

Interactive · WebGL Open visualizer →
02 · Nonlinear control

Cart–double-pendulum swing-up

Watch a cart drive two linked pendulums toward the unstable upright position, hand off to a balancing controller, or take over the actuator and attempt the swing-up yourself.

Interactive · Simulation Open visualizer →

Just for fun

Some games

These are small browser games built for play rather than mathematical demonstration. This seemed like the right place to share them.

01 · Lane defense

Rune Garden

Build a bright team of seed, frost, flame, and glow guardians across five lanes. Tap a defender, tap a tile, rescue threatened lanes, and evolve your favorites as increasingly strange creatures march toward the garden runes.

Interactive · Canvas Defend the garden →
02 · Action strategy

Linewarden

Hold the crystal with a mobile hero while enemy waves grow without stopping. Summon extra trouble into your own line to earn more gold every payday, then turn that risk into upgrades before the defenses break.

Interactive · Canvas Hold the line →
03 · Arcade game

Bouncy Ball Blaster

Move around a twelve-sided 3D arena, hit roaming targets, collect power-ups, and launch bouncing balls that can splat and split. This is an arcade game, not a mathematical demo.

Interactive · Three.js Enter playground →
